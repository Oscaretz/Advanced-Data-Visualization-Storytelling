#  Big Ideas — Action Plan Summary

##  Immediate Actions (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Audit current campaigns**  
   Calculate centrality scores (degree, closeness, betweenness, PageRank) for all promoted content.  
2. **Identify high-betweenness content**  
   Use these as bridges for cross-promotional campaigns targeting multiple audience segments.  
3. **Segment audiences by genre**  
   Create bridge campaigns that target overlapping genres to maximize cross-genre exposure.  
4. **Update targeting strategy**  
   Include “drama” or emotional storytelling keywords in every campaign, regardless of the primary genre.

---

##  Medium-Term Initiatives (Months 2–6)
1. **Build lookalike audiences by network distance**  
   Move beyond demographics—target users connected through shared content structures.  
2. **A/B test centrality vs. popularity campaigns**  
   Compare engagement and ROI between network-driven and traditional popularity-driven promotion.  
3. **Create “Genre Journey” campaigns**  
   Design marketing flows that guide users across 1–3 connected genres or titles.  
4. **Implement dynamic budget allocation**  
   Shift investment toward high-centrality content during awareness and reach phases.

---

##  Long-Term Strategy (Months 6–12)
1. **Influence content production**  
   Collaborate with content teams to design movies or series with intentional multi-genre positioning.  
2. **Develop a proprietary recommendation system**  
   Combine network centrality and collaborative filtering to personalize audience suggestions.  
3. **Build predictive centrality models**  
   Forecast how new content will rank in the network before launch, based on its genre mix.  
4. **Launch centrality-based influencer programs**  
   Match influencers to content network roles (hubs, bridges, connectors) instead of just follower count.

---

## 💰 Budget Distribution Guidelines
- **40%** → High-popularity content (conversion-focused campaigns)  
- **30%** → High-centrality content (audience expansion & awareness)  
- **20%** → High-betweenness content (cross-genre bridge campaigns)  
- **10%** → Niche/low-centrality content (long-tail experimentation)
